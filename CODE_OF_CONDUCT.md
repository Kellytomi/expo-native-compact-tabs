# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in `expo-native-compact-tabs` a
welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of identity, background, experience level, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements.
- Give and accept feedback with empathy.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sexualized attention,
trolling, threats, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a
professional community are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues, pull requests, code,
or other contributions that violate these standards. Repeated or serious
violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

To report conduct concerns privately, contact the maintainer through the
private contact methods listed on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Kellytomi). Reports will be
reviewed promptly and handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This policy applies in all project spaces and when an individual is publicly
representing the project.

This policy is informed by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/).
